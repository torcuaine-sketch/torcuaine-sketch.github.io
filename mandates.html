Mandates

A mandate is the written agreement behind a portfolio. It sets the objectives and the investment policy. It also fixes who decides. Under a discretionary mandate the manager decides alone. Under a non-discretionary one, each decision needs the client.

Ask about a mandate The published method

The two forms

Discretionary

The manager decides

Decisions stay within the mandate's investment policy. The client sets the objectives and the limits; reporting follows the mandate's terms.

Non-discretionary

The client confirms each decision

The manager prepares; the client decides. The mandate fixes the objectives, the policy and the reporting terms.

Execution and custody

Interactive Brokers executes and holds client portfolios.

What the client brings, and what comes back

The client brings the objectives, the constraints, and honesty about both. Back comes a written mandate, one consolidated statement of positions, risk and attribution, and a manager who says early when something will not hold.
