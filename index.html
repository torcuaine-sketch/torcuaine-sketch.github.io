Portfolio management under written mandates

Iceberg Portfolio Management is a Dubai-based portfolios-management house. Each portfolio runs under a written mandate: the objectives, the investment policy, and who decides. A dated research journal documents the working method.

How the research journal works

Every journal page names its sources. The studies price entries at the ask and exits at the bid. The full quoted spread lands as a cost in every result. Observation and interpretation stay apart. The open questions close each page. The session notes cover 28 July to 13 August 2026. They draw on exchange records and end-of-day option data.
