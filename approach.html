Approach

Portfolio management runs under written mandates. The research journal shows the working method on dated public records.

How a mandate runs

A mandate starts from the client's objectives, the investment policy and the decision rule. Under a discretionary mandate the manager decides within that policy. Under a non-discretionary mandate each decision needs the client. The mandate itself fixes the reporting terms.

How the universe is read

Instruments are classified by behaviour, not by label: how each acts when a regime turns, which shocks it transmits, and what it is actually correlated with when it matters. A sector name tells none of that.

Every position begins as a written thesis with a falsification test attached. A position without a stated way to be proven wrong does not get owned.

How decisions are made

A staged nightly cycle: regime, signals, blend, adversarial debate, optimisation, risk, then validation. Views are blended through Black-Litterman and hierarchical risk parity, so conviction is weighted against what the current regime allows rather than against how loudly it was argued.

Every allocation rule is validated on a rolling walk-forward window before it reaches production, and independent models argue against each allocation before capital is committed. Limits are enforced before a trade exists; the drawdown protocol decides while everyone is still calm.

Reconstruction pricing and sources

Each reconstruction names its instrument, its window and its fills basis. Each entry pays the ask; each exit takes the bid. The full quoted spread counts as a cost in every result. Fills cross the quoted end-of-day chain. The pages build on adjusted exchange records and end-of-day option chains. Each one closes with the open questions.

Session-note sources

Each session note names its instrument, its date, its price source and its implied-volatility source. Prices are the adjusted daily closes; implied volatility comes from end-of-day chains.

The journal studies public market history. The reconstructions are after-the-fact studies, not a track record. How to ask about a mandate is on the mandates page.
