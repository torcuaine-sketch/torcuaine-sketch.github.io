Risk disclosure

Investing places capital at risk. This page states the principal risks in plain terms.

Capital at risk

The value of investments falls as well as rises. A client may get back less than was invested, including substantially less. No return is promised, and nothing on this site should be read as a promise of one.

Past and hypothetical results

Past performance does not predict future results. The research journal's reconstructions are after-the-fact historical studies with stated assumptions; they are hypothetical, and they are not a record of firm activity.

Concentration and liquidity

Mandates may hold concentrated positions, and market liquidity varies. In stressed markets, exits take longer and cost more. Each mandate's own limits are set in the investment management agreement.

Currency and jurisdiction

Investments denominated in other currencies add exchange-rate risk. Legal and tax treatment depends on the client's own circumstances and can change.

One instruction

Invest only capital whose loss would not change essential plans, and read the mandate documents before signing them.
