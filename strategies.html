Strategies

Three strategies, each with a stated objective and rules that survive bad weeks. Mandate terms, minimums and liquidity are set out in the investment management agreement, not on this page.

Core Multi-Asset

Regime-aware global allocation. Views are blended through Black-Litterman and hierarchical risk parity, and drawdown control is treated as a first-class objective rather than an afterthought. This is the mandate most clients begin with.

Systematic Income

Credit and rates, tracked daily at the curve and spread level. Positioning aims at distribution stability, not headline yield.

Special Situations

Concentrated dislocation and event positions. Each position survives the full validation gauntlet before capital moves; size is capped and exits are pre-committed.

What a strategy is here

A written document: the objective, the universe, the sizing rules, the exit discipline. The research journal shows the same standards applied to public market history, with sources named.
