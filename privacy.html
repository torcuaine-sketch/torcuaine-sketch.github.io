Privacy

This page describes what the site collects, which is very little, and what happens when a visitor writes in.

What this site collects

Nothing on its own. The site sets no cookies, runs no scripts, and carries no analytics or tracking of any kind. Pages are static documents.

Hosting logs

The hosting infrastructure keeps standard technical logs, such as the requesting IP address and the requested page, for security and operations. These logs are not used to profile visitors.

When a visitor writes in

An email sent to the contact address arrives as ordinary email: the address, the content, and anything attached. That correspondence is kept as business records require, and is not sold or shared for marketing.

Client data

Once a mandate is discussed, identification and suitability information is collected as UAE regulation requires. That data is handled under the mandate's own terms and the applicable law, not under this page.
