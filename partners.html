Partners

Two arrangements. Introducers bring a relationship and keep it. External managers bring a strategy and keep the client.

Introducers

A referral arrangement for people with the relationships but not the licence: private bankers, brokers, lawyers, accountants, family-office staff.

The introducer brings one warm name and a call, to someone who qualifies as a professional client. There is no deck to learn, no product to explain, nothing to promise on the manager's behalf. The relationship stays with the introducer: each conversation's status is reported back, and the introduced client is never approached around the introducer.

No licence, no capital, no book to run, no operations, no compliance function is required of the introducer.

Introductions may only be made to clients who qualify as professional clients under applicable UAE rules; eligibility is assessed before any mandate is discussed. Introducers hold no licence of their own and do not give investment advice. Terms, including any fee sharing, are set out in a signed introducer agreement before the first name is passed, and are disclosed to the client in writing. Nothing on this page constitutes an offer of an introducer arrangement.

Division: external portfolio managers

A platform arrangement for managers with a process and a client base of their own. The manager keeps the process and the client relationship; the regulated wrapper, the execution rails and the risk layer are already built.

What the platform asks for: the strategy, run the manager's own way; the client relationship, with mandates issued in the manager's name rather than routed through a distributor; and a record that can be verified, audited or reconstructable, with the drawdowns left in.
