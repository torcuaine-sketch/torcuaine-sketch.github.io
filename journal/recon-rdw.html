RDW - historical reconstruction, 28 May to 16 Jul 2026

RDW, 28 May to 16 July 2026. A −67.4% retrace of a narrative advance, reconstructed from dated filings, exchange prints and end-of-day option quotes.

Type
Reconstruction
Window
28 May - 16 Jul 2026
Instrument
17 Jul 2026 15 put, single leg
Fills basis
End-of-day chains, crossed

Between 4 May and 28 May 2026, RDW rose from a close of 8.64 to 25.90 - roughly a tripling in eighteen sessions - three weeks after reporting results that missed on both lines. The advance then retraced in full.

Observed before

What was knowable beforehand

Every element below carries a date earlier than 2 June 2026, the first session of the studied expression.

First, the advance had no earnings support. Results published on 6 May 2026 missed on both lines: an EPS surprise of −12.5% and revenue −8.5%. The tripling from 8.64 (4 May) to 25.90 (28 May, roughly +200% in eighteen sessions) began after that miss. The move ran on narrative - a SpaceX-IPO reading applied across the space cohort - not on fundamentals.

Second, the sponsor had already left. Form 4 filings dated 10-24 April show AE RED HOLDINGS, a 10% owner with a board seat, selling approximately 33.7M shares at $9.3-10.9, culminating on 22 April with 21.37M shares sold for $231.8M and a residual position of 128 thousand shares. The largest holder exited essentially in full before the parabola began.

Third, the cohort top carried a date in public view. LUNR, PL and RDW peaked together on 27-28 May. SIDU priced a $100M direct offering into the 27 May peak - a monetisation of the mania, dated to the day - and press coverage had already turned ("Space Rally Hits a Wall", 19 May).

Fourth, the break itself carried dates and prices: the volume climax of 26-27 May (108M and 121M shares, the maxima of the series); the 29 May gap-down with a lower high (24.79 below 26.64); and the 1 June break of the rejection-day low at 21.66 with a close of 20.68 - a confirmed reversal, complete before the entry date.

A parabola that has lost its sponsor does not need a catalyst to end. It needs a session that fails.

Observed after

What happened

From 28 May to 16 July 2026 the stock fell from a closing price of 25.90 to 8.45, a decline of 67.4% on adjusted closes, with no split artefacts in the series. The decline was not an isolated event. The space cohort peaked in unison: LUNR at 45.70 and PL at 51.40 printed their highs on 27-28 May alongside RDW at 25.90, and all three reversed together. Volume in RDW climaxed at 108M and 121M shares on 26-27 May, the highest of the series. On 29 May the stock gapped lower and set a lower high, 24.79 against 26.64. On 1 June it broke below 21.66, the low of the rejection day, and closed at 20.68. By 16 July it closed at 8.45.

How a defined-risk expression behaved

The study takes a single long put: the 17 July 2026 monthly at a strike of 15, placed at the pre-parabola base (the 11-15 area), a listed strike quoted at a spread of 8.7% of mid at entry. The tenor left roughly six and a half weeks of runway.

The entry delta of approximately −0.18 derives from a recorded call delta of 0.8245. The exit spot of 8.41 comes from the end-of-day chain mark; the adjusted close that day read 8.45. At exit the bid of 6.20 sat 5.9% below intrinsic value of 6.59 (strike 15 less spot 8.41) - a normal crossing discount one day before expiry, with exercise at intrinsic available as the alternative, which makes the quoted fill conservative.

The volatility backdrop confirms the shape of the episode. The recorded 30-day implied volatility read 1.388 on 20 May, 1.642 at the 27 May peak, 1.535 at entry on 2 June and 1.037 at exit on 16 July - a classic blowoff pattern. That path is part of the case for a moderately out-of-the-money strike: an at-the-money put pays up for inflated volatility at exactly the wrong moment, while a strike at the pre-parabola base does not require the top and does not require a crash beyond the retrace.

Interpretation

The discipline

The trigger in this study is the confirmed break, not the peak. Selling into a parabola's high is a forecast; waiting for the gap-down, the lower high and the break of the rejection-day low is an observation. Entry on 2 June stood roughly 20% below the top, and the study suggests that cost little: parabolas that break after a sponsor's exit tend to retrace the entire move, so the structure did not need the peak to be relevant.

Two further points travel beyond this episode. A strike at the pre-parabolic base with six or more weeks of runway matches the anatomy of these retraces; far out-of-the-money strikes are not required, and at-the-money strikes overpay blowoff volatility. And a peer pricing an offering into the cohort's peak - SIDU's $100M on 27 May - is a datable marker of the top of the whole cohort, not merely of the issuing company.

Evidence boundary

Evidence notes

The known outcome selected this episode for study. Nothing in the selection was blind, and the reconstruction benefits from hindsight throughout.

The decay had no single dated catalyst. The dated facts - the insider exit, the earnings miss, the peer offering - are context; the trigger is the structural break, and the decline itself is best described as exhaustion rather than reaction to an event.

Quoted depth ran thin. At entry the quoted ask size was 1 contract against open interest of 578; at exit open interest stood at 37 with a bid size of 628. Treating the touch as available in size is a convention of the reconstruction, not an assurance.

The fills come from end-of-day marks. The signal completed at the 1 June close and the note marks entry at the 2 June end-of-day quote; the stock traded 20.3-22.15 intraday that session, so the convention is honest but still a convention. The chain and adjusted closing spots differ by 0.5% at exit (8.41 against 8.45), reflecting end-of-day snapshots taken at different times.

A second calculation refined two details without changing the picture: the Form 4 record shows 33.5M shares sold from a starting stake of roughly 33.6M at prices of 9.31-10.85, and the SIDU offering appears as a PRNewswire pricing release rather than an 8-K line item - the fact and the 27 May date stand. The implied-volatility series was re-verified against the chain summaries.
