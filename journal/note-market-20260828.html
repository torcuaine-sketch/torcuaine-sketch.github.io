Market data note (multi-instrument), 28 August 2026

Market overview, 28 August 2026. A daily note from public exchange records and end-of-day option data, covering AAPL, MSFT, NVDA, TSLA.

The 28 August 2026 session split the four-name universe cleanly in two, with only half the names finishing on the same side of unchanged and the broad market down 0.23%. Apple closed at 319.7, up 1.63% and exactly at its 20-day high, while Microsoft finished at 513.53, up 1.68% and likewise pinned to the top of its 20-day range. Both moves came against negative basket returns for their respective peer groups - residuals of roughly 0.022 and 0.039 - meaning the strength was idiosyncratic rather than a sector-wide lift, though neither residual registered as statistically extreme with 60-day z-scores near 1.1 to 1.2. Microsoft did it on volume 1.08 times its 20-day average; Apple on a lighter 0.92 times.

Nvidia was the clear separation point, closing at 217.55, down 4.57% on volume 1.55 times its 20-day average, and ending the session 4.57% below its 20-day high. The decline is striking set against its own news tape: on 27 August 2026 the company reported an earnings beat with forward guidance flagged as bullish across multiple items of materiality 7, alongside an unconfirmed report of a $12.9 billion acquisition. None of it held the price. The semiconductor group it sits in was flagged “stressed” for the first time on 28 August 2026, with median excess figures of -6.55 over 21 days and -6.39 over 10 days across 16 members, so the selling reads as industry-level pressure overwhelming a company-specific positive catalyst.

Tesla occupied the middle ground, closing at 348.75, down 1.71% on essentially average volume at 1.01 times its 20-day norm, sitting 3.89% below its 20-day high. Its basket residual of roughly -0.012 carried a z-score of only about -0.47, so the slip was unremarkable statistically, and its consumer discretionary group showed no rotation flag despite a -8.25 median excess over 21 days. Implied volatility told its own cross-sectional story: Tesla at 39.01% and Nvidia at 33.17% carried the highest 30-day readings in the universe, yet both sat near the bottom of their one-year ranges - ranks of 11.2 and 9.9 respectively - while Apple and Microsoft, at 23.61% and 24.74%, sat mid-range at 50.8 and 38.3.

The connective tissue this session is therefore less about direction than about what the moves were made of. The two advancers, Apple and Microsoft, rose on idiosyncratic residuals against weak peer baskets, but their trailing 30-day drift tells opposite stories: Apple’s drift z-score of -1.56 is the weakest in the universe while Microsoft’s 1.18 is the strongest, so Friday’s twin highs sit on very different foundations. Nvidia’s drop, by contrast, was mostly common-factor - its residual of roughly -0.016 against a -2.9% basket move implies it fell with its industry rather than against it, despite the positive guidance. Tesla’s -1.43 drift z-score places it alongside Apple in the weaker trailing cohort. Two names at range highs, one catalyst-rich laggard under a stressed group flag, and one quiet decliner define the dispersion.
