TSLA shares closed at 307.44 on Tuesday, July 28, 2026, a decline of 0.58 percent from the prior session's finish at 309.22, with roughly 39.4 million shares changing hands. The session marked the fourth consecutive day of lighter activity following an unusually heavy tape earlier in the period. Volume had peaked at approximately 115.6 million shares on July 23, when the stock fell from a close of 374.01 to 319.69, and turnover has tapered in each session since. The closing price has now drifted modestly lower across three straight sessions - 313.03 on July 24, 309.22 on July 27, and 307.44 most recently - while daily price swings have narrowed considerably relative to the prior week.

Options pricing reflected a still-elevated but cooling volatility backdrop. Ten-day implied volatility stood at 50.13 percent, while the thirty-day ex-earnings measure was 46.71 percent, indicating that near-term contracts carried a modest premium over the month-ago tenor even after removing event-related effects. The implied move for the near-dated expiry was 9.51 percent of the underlying price, a figure that remains wide in absolute terms though consistent with the sharp repricing observed during the prior week. A one-year implied volatility rank was not available in the dataset, leaving the current reading without a direct historical percentile comparison. The gap between the ten-day and ex-earnings thirty-day readings suggests that short-dated options continued to price meaningful event sensitivity around the equity.

The past five sessions describe a two-phase pattern for the stock: an abrupt repricing on July 23 accompanied by the heaviest volume of the period, followed by a quieter consolidation in a narrowing band between roughly 307 and 319. Average daily volume across the five sessions ran near 51.5 million shares, though that figure is heavily influenced by the July 23 spike; excluding that session, turnover clustered closer to 43 million shares per day. The cumulative move from the July 22 close of 374.01 to the July 28 close of 307.44 represents a decline of about 17.8 percent over four trading days. Whether the recent stabilization in both price and volume marks a durable base or a pause within a broader repricing is a question the tape itself does not yet answer.
