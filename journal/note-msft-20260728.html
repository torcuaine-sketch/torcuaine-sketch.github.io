MSFT closed at 393.35 on 2026-07-28, up 1.09% from the prior close of 389.10. Volume reached 32,367,546 shares, roughly 1% above the 20 day average and the heaviest turnover since 2026-07-16. The advance extended a rebound that began after the 2026-07-23 close at 381.58 and the nearly identical 2026-07-24 close at 381.70, which together marked the lower end of the recent pullback. Even with the gain, the stock remained 2.22% below its 20 day high of 402.29, set on 2026-07-20, while holding comfortably above the 20 day low of 373.02. The session therefore left price in the upper portion of its recent range without reclaiming the peak.

The last ten sessions describe a round trip rather than a clear trend. From 395.63 on 2026-07-15 the price rose to 401.10 on 2026-07-16 and reached the period high of 402.29 on 2026-07-20, then slipped across four consecutive declines to 381.58 by 2026-07-23. The two sessions that followed produced closes of 389.10 and 393.35, recovering a portion of that slide. Turnover was lighter during the descent, with prints of 24,126,632 on 2026-07-21 and 27,659,388 on 2026-07-24, and picked up as the price stabilized, reaching 32,367,546 in the latest session. Participation, in short, expanded alongside the rebound rather than during the earlier decline.

Thirty day implied volatility stood at 43.84%, down from 46.22% five sessions earlier, a decline of roughly 2.38 percentage points over that span. Even after that easing, the current reading sits at the 96.4th percentile of its one year range, so volatility remains elevated relative to the past twelve months even while cooling from the recent peak. The combination is notable: the equity price is about 2% under its recent high while implied volatility ranks near the top of its annual distribution. Volume was only marginally above its recent norm, which leaves the volatility picture as the more stretched of the two measures.
