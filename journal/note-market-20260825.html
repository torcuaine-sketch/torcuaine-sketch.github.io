Market data note (multi-instrument), 25 August 2026

Market overview, 25 August 2026. A daily note from public exchange records and end-of-day option data, covering AAPL, MSFT, NVDA, TSLA.

Market note

The broad market finished modestly higher on 25 August 2026, with the S&P 500 proxy up 0.32%, but the four megacap names in the coverage universe refused to move as a bloc. Nvidia led the group with a 2.19% advance, closing at 213.05 on volume running 1.08 times its 20-day average - the only name to trade on above-average participation. Microsoft rose 0.90% and Tesla added 0.37%, both on subdued turnover of 0.57 and 0.88 times their respective averages. Apple was the lone decliner, slipping 0.14% to 309.90 on volume at barely half its recent norm. The dispersion is the story: three of the four finished higher, yet the residuals behind those moves point in different directions, indicating that single-name catalysts rather than a shared sector impulse set the tone for the session.

Nvidia's advance is the more striking because it came against a semiconductor basket the data flags as stressed, with the group's median 21-day excess at -7.51 and median 10-day excess at -3.78, a designation first applied on 21 August 2026. The stock's session residual versus that weak basket was positive, and its 30-day drift is the second-strongest of the four names. The tape was supported by a dense news slate: a $20 billion acquisition of Groq reported on 24 August 2026, reported warnings to major customers of AI server price increases exceeding 15%, talks to invest in data-center power developer Cloverleaf Infrastructure, and an expansion of customer financing options announced on the session itself. Implied volatility of 35.46% sits well above the software and consumer electronics names here, yet a one-year rank of 23.6% shows options pricing remains low relative to the stock's own year.

Microsoft's 0.90% rise was quieter in headline terms but the firmest in trend terms. Its 30-day drift is the strongest of the four names, and its session residual was positive even though the software infrastructure basket around it declined on the day. The close of 491.71 leaves the shares just 2.84% below their 20-day high, the tightest positioning in the universe, while Apple sits 8.37% below its own. Volume at 0.57 times the 20-day average shows the move attracted little incremental participation, and implied volatility of 25.31% with a one-year rank of 40.8% is unremarkable. The group's median 10-day excess stands at -1.8 against a positive 3.26 over 21 days, so Microsoft's firmness reads as idiosyncratic resilience rather than confirmation of a group-level bid.

Apple and Tesla occupy the weaker end of the universe's 30-day drift ordering, and the session did little to change that. Apple's 0.14% decline came on its scheduled earnings date, yet the move was negligible, the residual versus its basket only modestly negative, and the turnover the lightest of the group in relative terms. Tesla presents the sharper divergence: its 0.37% rise masked a negative session residual, as the consumer discretionary basket around it advanced more strongly. Its news flow ran in both directions, with a recall of roughly 3 million vehicles in China and a regulatory mandate to fix them set against robotaxi operating permits granted in Las Vegas alongside Waymo and Uber. Tesla's implied volatility of 40.36% is the highest in the universe, though a one-year rank of 16.2% shows even that reading is subdued by its own history.
