CAR - historical reconstruction, 25 Mar to 8 May 2026

Avis Budget Group (CAR), 25 March to 8 May 2026. A reconstruction of a call-driven squeeze in a vehicle-rental company and of its unwinding, assembled from dated exchange records, Form 4 filings and end-of-day option quotations.

Type
Reconstruction
Window
25 Mar - 8 May 2026
Instrument
CAR May-2026 monthly 300 put
Fills basis
End-of-day chains, crossed

Over the up-leg CAR rose 477.8 per cent on adjusted closes, from 123.56 to 713.97 between 25 March and 21 April. Over the down-leg it fell 79.6 per cent, from 713.97 to 145.75 by 8 May. From the reconstruction's entry at the close of 22 April, at 443.94, the decline to 8 May ran 67.2 per cent. This note examines which parts of that reversal carried a date before it happened, and how the market quoted one defined-risk option structure through it.

Observed before

What was knowable beforehand

Every fact above carries a date on or before the close of 22 April 2026, and the reconstruction enters through the end-of-day chain after the reversal bar completed. The trigger is the bar itself - a parabola, an IV rank of 100, record volume, and a close far below the prior close: a dated, replicable fact, not a story. The volatility readings and the Pentwater filings serve as context around that trigger, not the trigger itself, and nothing in the entry requires the backfit of a narrative.

The squeeze mechanics stood equally public in advance. A ten-per-cent holder exercising $322 million of calls at strikes of 110 to 130, filed on 19, 20 and 24 March, describes the manufacture of the advance; the IV rank of 100 from 7 April describes how crowded it had become; and the 29 April earnings date, on the calendar ahead, supplied the event horizon the structure was built through.

Observed after

What happened

Between 25 March and 21 April the stock rose 478 per cent in nineteen sessions with no dated fundamental catalyst; the February report, on 18 February, had shown an earnings surprise of −2,152 per cent - a miss that did nothing to support the rally. What the public record did show: the mechanics. Form 4 filings by Pentwater Capital, a ten-per-cent holder, filed on 19, 20 and 24 March, disclosed the exercise of calls worth roughly $322 million at strikes of 110 to 130: the fuel of a call-driven squeeze, visible before the run.

Implied volatility rose with the price - thirty-day implied volatility went from 87 on 2 April to 244.6 on 21 April, with the one-year IV rank pinned at 100 from 7 April - a squeeze signature rather than ordinary event pricing. 21 April printed the climax bar: up 17 per cent to close at 713.97, with an intraday high of 765.94, on record volume of 12.3 million shares. 22 April was the key reversal: a gap up to 847.70, then a close at 443.94 - 37.8 per cent below the prior close - on 12.2 million shares. Ahead sat a dated catalyst: earnings on 29 April, which in the event showed an earnings surprise of −17.4 per cent. By 8 May the stock closed at 145.75.

How a defined-risk expression behaved

The study takes a single long CAR May-2026 monthly 300 put - a back month spanning the 29 April report, roughly seventeen delta out of the money. A single long put, no spread. The structure risks its premium and nothing beyond it.

Both fills cross the complete quotation - the ask on entry, the bid on exit. At entry the spread measured 14.9 per cent of mid, with monotonic neighbouring strikes; the staleness check passes. At exit, with eight days to expiry, intrinsic value stood at 152.58 against a bid of 146.40: the bid sits just below parity, so the quotation is conservative - exercise would have yielded slightly more. Between entry and exit, thirty-day implied volatility collapsed from about 210 per cent to 66, and the 68 per cent decline in the underlying outweighed the crush several times over. The two rows can be set side by side without further commentary.

Interpretation

The discipline

A blow-off reversal bar - parabola, IV rank at 100, record volume, close well below the prior close - is a dated, replicable short trigger. Puts remain serviceable even at 210 per cent implied volatility, because once the puts move deep into the money the spot move dominates the volatility crush. Strike selection is the decision that matters: across at-the-money, seventeen-delta, ten-delta and five-delta puts through the same decline, the moderate wing - ten to twenty delta - responded most efficiently, while deeper strikes behaved as lottery tickets.

In a squeeze, trade the resolution, not the run-up.

The second rule: no exit on the first bounce. 27 April offered an early exit; the dated catalyst was 29 April and the structure ran through it, so the reconstruction holds to 8 May. The up-leg carries the humility lesson. The only early dated hints on the long side were the Pentwater Form 4 filings and the 23-24 March breakout above 105, and a parabola of this scale cannot be forecast. The long leg was nearer to luck; the short after the reversal was knowable.

Evidence boundary

Evidence notes

What follows rebuilds a move that was already history. The name entered the series because the move happened; the page makes no claim about how often such setups resolve badly. Specific caveats. The end-of-day chain spot on 22 April (457.25) differs from the adjusted close (443.94) by about three per cent - a chaotic session and composite snapshots; the chain's premiums are consistent with the chain spot. The reconstruction assumes the full bid/ask crossing, but real slippage on 22 April, in puts at implied volatility above 200 per cent, could have been worse than the quotes. The exit bid of 146.40 sat below the intrinsic value of 152.58; exercise would have yielded slightly more, so the study is conservative on this leg as well.

The 29 April earnings date finds confirmation only after the fact; that the schedule was published before 22 April is standard practice but is not verified from dated records. The dated record used here identifies no local news for CAR, so the “rental squeeze” context is not documented by dated news; the squeeze mechanics rest on the Form 4 and volatility evidence alone. Pentwater's sales of $1.75 billion at prices between 438 and 702 on 22-23 April reached the filings on 28 April - after the entry; they confirm the thesis but play no part in it. Finally, the volatility signature for CAR uses per-date readings.
