NBIS - historical reconstruction, 30 Mar to 18 Jun 2026

Nebius Group (NBIS), 30 March to 18 June 2026. A reconstruction of a structural break in a liquid AI-infrastructure name, assembled from dated exchange records and end-of-day option quotations.

Type
Reconstruction
Window
30 Mar - 18 Jun 2026
Instrument
NBIS Jun-2026 monthly 160 call
Fills basis
End-of-day chains, crossed

Over the window NBIS rose 210.7 per cent on adjusted closes, from 92.26 to 286.69. The note tests which pieces of the move had dates in advance and tracks the quotations of one defined-risk option structure.

Observed before

What was knowable beforehand

Every element of the entry case carries a date on or before 10 April 2026, the session the reconstruction uses as its entry point - the morning after the break itself. The October all-time-high close of 135.46 stood public from 13 October 2025. The panic low of 92.26 on 30 March and the 9 April close at 136.33 above it - the first close through the old high, on volume that had risen three sessions running - were in the record before entry. The signal is the break; the entry is the next session, not the low.

A catalyst with a date waited further down the line. The reporting cadence - 7 August 2025, 11 November 2025, 12 February 2026 - placed the next quarterly report around mid-May, and the June expiry choice deliberately covered it. It in fact arrived on 13 May, with an earnings surprise of +70 per cent and a gap of +13.8 per cent. The dated context was consistent: NVIDIA's $2.2 billion investment in Nebius was public before entry - news coverage of 8 May refers to it as an accomplished fact - and the AI-infrastructure ramp showed revenue up 684 per cent year on year. Notably, the failed 12 February report (an earnings surprise of −70 per cent) had not prevented a return to the all-time high within six weeks; demand was absorbing bad news.

The records used here do not establish a volatility signature, so the volatility branch is unverifiable and the classification rests on structure. Judged roughly from the at-the-money premium at around 70 days to expiry, implied volatility sat near 70 per cent - expensive, but normal for the name.

Observed after

What happened

The local daily record puts the prior all-time-high close at 135.46, on 13 October 2025. The stock fell to a low of 92.26 on 30 March 2026 and recovered in a V: on 9 April it closed at 136.33 - the first close above the October high - completing an eight-day recovery on rising volume, 18.7 million shares building to 20.4 million and then 25.8 million against a base of 11 to 14 million.

The first-quarter report arrived on 13 May, with an earnings surprise of +70 per cent and a gap of +13.8 per cent on 38.7 million shares. The advance ran into expiry week: on 18 June, the boundary of the study window and the expiry date of the June monthly series, the stock gapped open at 298.20 and closed at 286.69 on 36.6 million shares - elevated, but not a record: NBIS printed 88.4 million shares on 9 September 2025, and the earnings day of 13 May itself traded 38.8 million.

How a defined-risk expression behaved

The study takes a single long NBIS June-2026 monthly 160 call. The June monthly expired on Thursday 18 June rather than the customary Friday, because of the Juneteenth holiday on 19 June; at entry it carried 70 days to expiry and comfortably spanned the dated 13 May report. The most the structure can give up is its premium.

Entry takes the quoted ask whole; exit takes the quoted bid whole. The entry quotes are healthy: the ask at $17.95 sits nowhere near a stale or nominal market, the spread measured 5.7 per cent of mid, and the neighbouring strikes ran monotonic - no staleness suspicion. At exit the contract was deep in the money on its expiry day: with the chain spot at 285.04, intrinsic value was 125.04, and the exit took the bid of 123.30, 1.74 below intrinsic - an honest full crossing; exercise would have yielded roughly 1.7 points more. The two rows, read together, carry the point unaided.

Interpretation

The discipline

The episode turns on one narrow, repeatedly useful habit of craft. An all-time-high break after a V-recovery, in a liquid name with a dated report inside the option's life, argues for buying the at-the-money monthly call that covers the catalyst - not for waiting for a pullback. The entry in this reconstruction sits 58 per cent above the window low (145.58 against 92.26), and the structure still did its work, because a call monetises acceleration, not the bottom. Nothing in the quote history above depends on having caught 92.26; the reconstruction enters on the day after the evidence dated itself.

The expiry must outlive the catalyst with margin. Time makes the drawdown survivable.

The second point: the term of the contract. Seventy days to expiry allowed the structure to sit through the drawdown of 5 to 10 June - spot from 264 to 211, on which the call lost roughly half its value from the local peak - without a forced exit, and to be present for the final leg into expiry. A shorter series covering the same report would have put that decision on the holder at the worst moment of the move.

Evidence boundary

Evidence notes

Here the study rebuilds a move that had already taken place. Occurrence of the move, not any forecast, selected this name - and the failure rate of comparable setups stays unestimated. The exit date is the expiry of the instrument, not a signal.

Specific caveats. The volatility branch could not be verified, so the classification rests solely on structure. The mid-May report date was not on record in advance; its knowability is inferred from the cadence of past quarters. The intra-trade drawdown was severe (264 on 1 June to 211 on 10 June, with the call down roughly half from its local peak); the study modelled no stop or trailing rule, and a less patient version of the same position could have left before the final leg - that it did not is partly a property of the reconstruction, not of the setup. The exit at the bid of 123.30 sat 1.74 below intrinsic value of 125.04, a conservative crossing. Finally, the chain's exit spot of 285.04 differs from the adjusted close of 286.69 by roughly 0.6 per cent, a snapshot-timing artefact.
