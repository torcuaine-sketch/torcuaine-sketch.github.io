Ownership and structure

The portfolio is one layer. Most of what protects a family sits above it: how the assets are owned, who inherits them, which authority reads the statements.

Iceberg does not practise law. The mandate is built to fit the structure the client's counsel designs, and a structure that will not hold gets said early.

Succession and wills

A DIFC or ADGM will, or one in the home jurisdiction, drafted by the client's lawyers. What changes on the mandate side is how it is titled and who may instruct it. Settled before funding, not after.

The ownership vehicle

Foundation, trust, holding company or personal name. Instructions come from whoever signs for the vehicle, and the reporting follows that entity rather than the person who opened the conversation.

Residency and substance

Tax residency drives what gets reported and where; the manager's address does not change it. Advisers receive the records they need, in the format they ask for.

Reporting obligations

CRS and FATCA classification, annual statements, custodian paperwork, and the confirmations auditors ask for once a year without warning.
